# Phase 0 letter to the owner

A Chinese version is in [zh-CN/letters/2026-09.md](../zh-CN/letters/2026-09.md).

**The bad news first.**

- The APP archive went through its first independent fact audit: of 237 facts, seven were wrong, which were really two problems. First, debt principal and net debt copied the rounded figures in your report; those figures came from remarks on an earnings call and don't match the filings. Second, the starting point for the change in share count was taken from the wrong year. In one more place, numbers from before and after a restatement were used together. All of this has been corrected against the SEC originals and recorded in the public mistakes list. The root cause is that SEC data could not yet be reached when the archive was built, so the numbers were not traced back to the primary filings one by one. The archives of the other five companies have not had the same audit yet.
- Your own company reports also have a few places that don't match the primary filings: SPGI's billed issuance for the first half, PDD's 2025 profit basis, APP's average buyback price and others. The archives always follow the primary filings; the report text is left unchanged, and the differences are logged in the errata table in the private repository.
- The private valuations of five companies each have one method issue awaiting review (details in the private files). The most important is Berkshire's, because its long-term return estimate is the first hurdle for every company. Under the rules, no valuation number was changed this time; they will be recalculated at the next valuation refresh and take effect only after independent review.
- One branch of one of APP's breaker tests cannot be resolved for now: there is no list of "top publishers" that can be cited publicly and frozen. Until one is found, that branch is recorded as "undetermined" every quarter, and the other branch is resolved as usual.
- Two automatic checks were stricter than the original text of your constitution: one locked the target position inside the entry bar, and the other took the higher of Berkshire and VOO as the hurdle. Both have been corrected to follow the original text: the entry bar is a bar, not a cap, and a higher-conviction position can be larger, with the reasons written down; Berkshire is the first hurdle and VOO the second.

**The biggest uncertainty.** APP has not yet announced the release date of its third-quarter results. The pre-registration deadline is the end of the day before the release date, and it must be merged and timestamped at least 72 hours earlier; companies usually announce the date a few weeks before the release, so the deadline can only be fixed then.

**For your attention.**

1. Review the three repositories. Once you confirm, I will create them on GitHub and push: thesis-ci and owners-office public, owners-office-private private.
2. Put the model API key into GitHub Secrets under the name `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. Only you can do this; don't paste it into a chat or into code.

**What this phase did.** The three repositories were set up and committed for the first time. The investment constitution was organized from your original text into thirteen rules and five hard rules, each mapped to an automatic check. The duties of the thirteen roles and the materials each of them can see were fixed, and the full prompt texts are kept only in the private repository. The six companies' theses, thesis tests, two-minute stories and sources tables were migrated to the new format, and every SEC filing they cite now has its accession number. Model calls have a single entry point: inputs are isolated by role, and structured outputs are validated before they are written to disk. All seven Phase 0 acceptance criteria passed.

**Decisions made (reported).**

- Holdings APP and PDD; candidates AXP, MSFT and SPGI; BRK is archived and also serves as the source of the first hurdle (you confirmed this).
- The prompts stay in the private repository for now, and the public repository cites them only by number (you confirmed this).
- Prices are graded on the MSFT version of the scale you chose, without plus or minus signs; on this scale, two companies each moved down one step (details in the private files).
- The discount rate equals the 10-year Treasury yield plus a premium judged from the company's own cash-flow record, with no comparison or interpolation between companies.
- Public files contain no multiples that take the current share price as an input, price-to-book included; the thresholds of Berkshire's two related tests are kept only in private files.
- Trust levels are scored from the pipeline's first output; the archives built by hand in Phase 0 are not scored, but the APP errors found this time still go into the mistakes list.
- SEC access uses the contact email you gave, written only into the request header and never into any repository; commits use the GitHub noreply address.
- Source tags for your own reports now consist of "ticker, report number, date", so that old tags don't get frozen into the pre-registration timestamps; all of them have been changed.
