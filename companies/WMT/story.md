# Walmart (WMT): the two-minute story

**What it does.** Walmart sells groceries and general merchandise through more than 10,900 stores and clubs and its websites [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item1]. The two U.S. segments produced about 82% of FY2026 net sales [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item1A], and grocery was 59% of Walmart U.S. net sales [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note11].

**Why it is a good business.** Buying volume and a dense U.S. distribution network let it charge less and still earn a Walmart U.S. segment margin of 5.2% in FY2026 [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item7]. Suppliers fund the inventory: accounts payable were 107% of inventories at 2026-01-31 [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item8]. The stores now double as warehouses for online orders, and eCommerce reached 21% of FY2026 net sales [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note11]. The traffic is increasingly sold as advertising and memberships; membership fee revenue rose from $3.1bn in FY2024 to $4.4bn in FY2026 [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note1].

**What is not yet good.** Capital spending rose from about 2.1% of total revenues in FY2017–FY2019 to about 3.5% in FY2024–FY2026, while free cash flow per diluted share fell about 15% between those two periods [src:WMT-10K-FY2019#IS] [src:WMT-10K-FY2019#CF] [src:WMT-10K-FY2021#IS] [src:WMT-10K-FY2021#CF] [src:WMT-10K-FY2025#p58] [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item8].

**What must go right.** Advertising, membership and marketplace income must outgrow depreciation, wages, healthcare and liability costs, so that the spending turns into more free cash per share; and the after-tax return on invested capital must hold near its FY2026 level of 14.5% [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item8] [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note5] [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note6] [src:WMT-10K-FY2025#BS].

**What would prove it wrong.** A Walmart U.S. segment margin below 4% in two straight fiscal years, against 5.2% in FY2026 [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item7]; an after-tax return on invested capital below 11% in two straight years, against 14.5% in FY2026, computed from the FY2026 and FY2025 statements [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Item8] [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note5] [src:WMT-10K-FY2026#Note6] [src:WMT-10K-FY2025#BS]; negative Walmart U.S. comparable sales in two straight years; or any three years in which free cash flow does not cover the dividend.
