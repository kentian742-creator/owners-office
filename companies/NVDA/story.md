# NVIDIA (NVDA): the two-minute story

**What it does.** NVIDIA designs AI data-center computers: GPUs, CPUs, networking and the CUDA software that runs on them. Contract manufacturers build them [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Item1]. Data Center was 92.5% of FY2027Q2 revenue [src:NVDA-10Q-FY2027Q2#Note13]. Cloud companies, AI clouds and AI labs buy these systems to train and run models.

**Why it is a good business.**
- In FY2026 it turned 60.4% of revenue into operating income [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Item7].
- It spent 2.8% of revenue on property, equipment and intangibles [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#CF][src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#IS], or 9.5% including the $13.0B paid for the Groq technology license and $1.5B of acquisitions [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#CF][src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#IS][src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Note2].
- It moved from selling Hopper HGX systems to whole Blackwell data-center systems [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Item7]. It still earned a 75.0% gross margin in FY2027Q2 [src:NVDA-8K-2026-08-26#EX-99.1]. So customers pay for the integration and the software, not only for the chip.
- Over 7.5 million developers use CUDA and NVIDIA tools [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Item1].

**Why it is not an easy hold.**
- Profits are cyclical. Operating income fell 25% in FY2020 and 58% in FY2023 [src:NVDA-10K-FY2020#Item7][src:NVDA-10K-FY2023#Item7].
- Two direct customers took 22% and 14% of FY2026 revenue [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Note16].
- NVIDIA now backs its customers' buildouts with its own balance sheet:
  - guarantees, signed in August 2026, capped at $105B, covering an OpenAI affiliate's lease and power payments [src:NVDA-8K-2026-08-17#Item1.01];
  - $36B of AI-cloud commitments [src:NVDA-10Q-FY2027Q2#Note10];
  - payment terms of up to a year [src:NVDA-10Q-FY2027Q2#Note7].

**What must go right.**
1. Gross margin stays above 70% (75.0% in FY2027Q2 [src:NVDA-8K-2026-08-26#EX-99.1]) against custom chips built by its largest customers [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Item1].
2. AI spending keeps spreading to AI clouds, enterprises and sovereign buyers, so that mid-cycle revenue stays far above FY2023's $26,974M [src:NVDA-10K-FY2023#Item7].
3. Management keeps that customer support small next to the cash the business generates.

**What would prove it wrong.** Any one of these:
- gross margin below 60% for two quarters (75.0% in FY2027Q2 [src:NVDA-10Q-FY2027Q2#IS]);
- Data Center revenue down 30% or more year on year for two quarters (up 117% in FY2027Q2, $89,023M against $41,096M [src:NVDA-10Q-FY2027Q2#Note13][src:NVDA-8K-2026-08-26#EX-99.2]);
- inventory provisions above 10% of a year's revenue (3.3% in FY2026, $7.2B on $215.9B [src:NVDA-10K-FY2026#Item7]);
- more than $5B paid out under lease guarantees (no payment disclosed through FY2027Q2, against $108.5B of maximum gross exposure [src:NVDA-10Q-FY2027Q2#Note10]);
- debt above cash and marketable debt securities.
