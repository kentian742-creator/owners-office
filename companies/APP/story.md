**What it does.** AppLovin runs MAX, an "auction house" for mobile-game ads: each ad slot is auctioned in real time, with outside buyers such as Meta, Google and Unity bidding against its own Axon model [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p3]. Axon bids for advertisers, pricing dynamically to each advertiser's return target [src:APP-10K-FY2025#Item1]. Since selling all its game studios in 2025, all continuing-operations revenue comes from advertising [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p4].

**Why it is a good business, and why not yet first-rate.** Q2 2026 revenue was $1.924 billion, up 53% year on year, with a 77.7% operating margin [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p3]. Trailing four-quarter return on invested capital to 2026-06-30 was about 124%–135% [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p6]; 2025 PP&E purchases were only $470,000, and only about $19 million including finance-lease principal, about 0.35% of revenue [src:APP-8K-2026-02-11#EX-99.1]. But the lead is not structural: supply and training data are bought, only the model is its own, and the lead must be re-earned every quarter by beating rivals [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p6]. All growth comes from net revenue per install, whose year-on-year growth slowed from +93% in Q1 2026 to +58% in Q2 [src:APP-10Q-FY2026Q1#Item2][src:APP-10Q-FY2026Q2#Item2], while Unity's Vector grew 23% sequentially in Q2 (per Unity's earnings call) [src:U-CALL-FY2026Q2]. Hence the report's verdict: "ordinary business × excellent management" [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p10].

**What has to go right.** In Q3 and Q4 2026, under the new CTO: revenue at or above the guidance midpoint, sequential growth re-accelerating in the Q4 peak season, and either installs back to growth or net revenue per install growing 30% or more [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p1]. E-commerce advertisers must become a measurable second curve: consumer spend in Q2 2026 was already 28% above Q4 2025 (per the company's earnings call) [src:APP-CALL-FY2026Q2#CEO].

**What would prove me wrong.** Advertising revenue up less than 15% year on year for two straight quarters, with install growth negative [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p15]; TTM owner free cash flow margin below 45% for two straight quarters (about 62% as of 2026-06-30) [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p15]; regulators or courts ordering the auction house split from the in-house buyer, a platform policy change that badly hurts Axon, or a finding that its data collection violates privacy law or platform terms; key people leaving without an internal successor, or MAX publisher revenue falling sequentially for two straight quarters [src:APP-RPT1-2026-09-23#p15].
