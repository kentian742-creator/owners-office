**What it does:** Microsoft rents Microsoft 365 (with the cloud portion of Windows Commercial), Dynamics and LinkedIn's Talent and Sales Solutions to businesses around the world by the seat, and rents out computing power through Azure [src:MSFT-10K-FY2026#p5]. FY2026 revenue was $331.8 billion, with an operating margin of 46.8% [src:MSFT-10K-FY2026#p50].

**Why it is a good business:** In the segment holding the seat business, nearly sixty cents of every dollar of FY2026 revenue was operating profit [src:MSFT-10K-FY2026#p38], and a customer who wants to replace it has to migrate its files, reconnect identity and rebuild compliance [src:MSFT-RPT1-2026-09-17#p3]. Companies buy Azure through the same contract and the same salesperson [src:MSFT-RPT1-2026-09-17#p3]; Azure's revenue passed $100 billion for the first time in FY2026 [src:MSFT-8K-2026-07-29#EX-99.1].

**What has to go right:** For the first time, it is putting its annuity's cash into data centers on a large scale: FY2026 cash capex was $115.9 billion, 34.9% of revenue, and free cash flow fell to 52.0% of non-GAAP net income [src:MSFT-10K-FY2026#p53] [src:MSFT-10K-FY2026#p42]. The spending has to buy profitable growth, the capex cycle has to come down, and the $678 billion commercial backlog at 2026-06-30 [src:MSFT-10K-FY2026#p77] has to be backed by customers other than OpenAI, whose share was about 45% at the only disclosure (FY26 Q2, of $625 billion) [src:MSFT-CALL-FY2026Q2].

**What would prove me wrong:** Azure constant-currency growth below 20% for two straight quarters while cash capex is still above 25% of revenue, FY2028 cash conversion below 45% on that year's non-GAAP definition, Microsoft Cloud gross margin below 58% for two straight quarters, RPO growth excluding OpenAI below 5% or the $250 billion commitment [src:MSFT-8K-2025-10-29#EX-99.2] restructured or impaired, or seats starting to fall while the company cannot show growing agent or usage revenue [src:MSFT-RPT1-2026-09-17#p11] [src:MSFT-RPT1-2026-09-17#p12]. Three more have to be read from documents: Nadella leaving with no disclosed succession plan, or the 2027 proxy's long-term incentives still lacking a return-on-capital measure while cash capex is still above $150 billion [src:MSFT-RPT1-2026-09-17#p12]; an impairment of Microsoft's OpenAI stake, or OpenAI unable to fund its compute commitments; and, once the EU's Cloud and AI Development Act takes effect, the Level 3 third-country recognition route closed to US providers in practice [src:MSFT-RPT1-2026-09-17#p12].
