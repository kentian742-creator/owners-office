# Costco (COST): the two-minute story

**What it does.** Costco runs 939 membership warehouses, 762 of them in the U.S., Puerto Rico and Canada [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item2]. Members pay an annual fee to buy from a narrow, bulk assortment, at prices the company describes as consistently lower than elsewhere [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item1]. In FY2026 membership fees came to $5,907 m, about half of the $11,685 m of operating income [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item8].

**Why it is a good business.** Costco passes its purchasing scale back to members as price, and the extra volume makes the scale larger. Gross margin stayed between 10.48% and 11.35% of net sales in every year from FY2014 to FY2026 [src:COST-10K-FY2016#Item7] [src:COST-10K-FY2018#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2020#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2022#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2024#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item8]. Profit therefore grew through cost leverage, not through a wider markup on goods [src:COST-10K-FY2016#Item7] [src:COST-10K-FY2018#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2020#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2022#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2024#IS] [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item8]. Members accept the fee: the U.S. and Canada renewal rate was 92.3% at the end of FY2026, after a fee increase [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item1]. Suppliers' payables fund the inventory, so the pretax return on capital was 53.7% in FY2026 [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item8].

**What must go right.** Management, new at the top since January 2024, must keep the markup thin [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item1]. Members who join online and abroad must renew like those who joined in a warehouse. New warehouses, planned to add about 3% to the base each year, must keep ramping toward the mature average [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item7] [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item5].

**What would prove it wrong.** Any one of four events would:

- the U.S. and Canada renewal rate, 92.3% at the end of FY2026 [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item1], below 90% at two straight year-ends;
- fewer paid members than a year earlier;
- comparable traffic negative for four straight quarters;
- operating income, 3.85% of total revenue in FY2026 [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item8], below 3% of revenue for two straight years.

One reading has already weakened: paid member growth slowed from 6.3% in FY2025 to 3.8% in FY2026 [src:COST-10K-FY2026#Item1].
