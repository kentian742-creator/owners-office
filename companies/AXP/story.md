**What it does.** American Express is card issuer, payment network and merchant acquirer at once. Merchants pay a discount fee on card spending, members pay an annual fee, and unpaid balances earn interest. In fiscal 2025 card members spent $1.6698 trillion [src:AXP-10K-FY2025#p43], and of $72.229 billion in revenue, merchant discount was 51.8% and annual fees (net card fees) 13.8% [src:AXP-10K-FY2025#p92]. It is a Lynch stalwart, with fiscal 2025 revenue up 9.5% (10% as the company rounds it) [src:AXP-10K-FY2025#p43]; it is also cyclical, because at bottom it is a spread lender with a subscription layer on top [src:AXP-RPT1-2026-09-17#p4].

**Why it is a good business.** The annual fee itself costs almost nothing at the margin, but keeping members takes ever-richer benefits [src:AXP-RPT1-2026-09-17#p5]. Net card fees grew from $988 million in Q2 2019 to $2.86 billion in Q2 2026 [src:AXP-10Q-FY2019Q2#p1] [src:AXP-10Q-FY2026Q2#p39], through pandemic shutdowns, zero rates, rate hikes and rate cuts: that is pricing power, not a tailwind [src:AXP-RPT1-2026-09-17#p9]. The customer base is also more reliable: Amex's Q2 2026 net write-off rate (principal only, consumer and small business) was 2.0% [src:AXP-10Q-FY2026Q2#p8], including proceeds from a June sale of written-off debt [src:AXP-8K-2026-09-15], about 1.9 points below the 3.92% on all U.S. commercial banks' credit card loans [src:FRB-CHGOFF-2026-08-25].

**What has to go right.** Fees have to catch up with the benefit costs that came with the price increases: in the first half of 2026 each extra dollar of card fees brought about $1.60 of extra benefit costs [src:AXP-10Q-FY2026Q2#p40], and the first clean reading comes in Q2 2027. The Delta co-brand, about 13% of billed business, has to be renewed before it expires at the end of 2029 [src:AXP-10K-FY2025#p4].

**What would prove me wrong.** Card-fee growth in single digits in Q2 2027 while benefit costs still grow 30% or more, the industry charge-off rate back at or above its 2024 peak while Amex builds reserves for two straight quarters, AI platforms' default rules choosing cards by total cost without letting users lock in a card, or no Delta renewal announced by the end of 2028, a renewal that loses exclusivity, or remuneration growing faster than Amex's revenue for two straight years [src:AXP-RPT1-2026-09-17#p14].
