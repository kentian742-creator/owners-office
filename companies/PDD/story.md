**What it does.** Pinduoduo and Temu are two copies of the same mechanism, one at home and one abroad: aggregate demand, hand large orders to Chinese factories, and trade low prices for scale [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p6] [src:PDD-RPT1-2026-09-16#p4]. The platforms earn no margin on goods and collect only two kinds of money: the advertising fees merchants pay for traffic (online marketing) and transaction service fees, each line about half of revenue in 2025 [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p164] [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p101].

**Why it is a good business.** It runs on merchants' money: at the end of June 2026, payables to merchants plus merchant deposits formed a float of RMB 128.5 billion, and operating invested capital was negative RMB 182.9 billion (calculated by classifying balance-sheet line items), with no interest-bearing debt [src:PDD-6K-2026-08-25#EX-99.1]. From 2020 to 2025, the sales and marketing expense ratio fell from 69.2% to 29.0%, while revenue grew 7.3-fold over the same period [src:PDD-6K-2022-03-22#EX-99.1] [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p153].

**What has to go right.** Three things, none of them in the business itself. Domestic ranking power has to be held: online marketing revenue must not be taken away by content commerce and shopping agents, yet in Q2 2026 this line (group level) grew only 3.5% [src:PDD-6K-2026-08-25#EX-99.1]. Cash needs a channel to reach shareholders, yet eight years after listing there have been zero dividends and zero buybacks [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p126] [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p17] [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p140] [src:PDD-20F-FY2025#p157] [src:PDD-WEB-2026-09-24-1]. The relationship with regulators must not get worse: in 2026 two sets of regulations banned the technical means of its low-price mechanism [src:PDD-RPT1-2026-09-16#p11], and two fines total about RMB 3.1 billion [src:SAMR-CF-2026-22] [src:EC-IP-26-1178] [src:CFETS-CCPR-2026-05-28].

**What would prove me wrong.** Online marketing revenue down year on year for four straight quarters, the operating margin below 15% for four straight quarters, or the merchant float down year on year for four straight quarters [src:PDD-RPT1-2026-09-16#p26]. The VIE ruled invalid, or Temu barred from operating in either the US or the EU [src:PDD-RPT1-2026-09-16#p26]. Xinpinmu, if it is indeed self-operated, ending in inventory write-downs or a silent exit [src:PDD-RPT1-2026-09-16#p26].
