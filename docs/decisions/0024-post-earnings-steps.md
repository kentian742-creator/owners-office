# 0024 Post-earnings steps: the event's documents, the evaluation of the quantitative tests, the placement actions of §F2, the holdings' steps, HQ's gate and the event chain

## Background

0019 built the runner for the steps before an earnings event (14Q, 15A) and for the monthly letter (18), and listed what the steps after an event would need. The first event of the season is American Express's FY2026Q3 results, expected around 2026-10-14; AXP is a candidate, so its quarterly update is due within 7 days of the filing's acceptance. The holdings follow: AppLovin around 2026-11-02 and PDD around 2026-11-13.

Per the design and the prompts' scopes, a candidate's event gets the quantitative tests and the company manager's update (03) with its fact audit (16A → 04A → 03R); a holding's event also gets the independent judge (14T), the blind read (14A), the red team's inversion list (04B-lite), the divergence map (14B), the settlement of due predictions (15B) and HQ's release gate (17A). The work is split into Phase A (the candidate path, needed by 2026-10-12) and Phase B (the holdings' steps, needed by 2026-10-28).

Questions the design and 0019 leave open:

- Which documents a post-event step reads: `latest_filings` (15A) is the quarter before the one being pre-registered; the steps after an event need that event's own documents, and only once the event has closed (03: the 10-Q/10-K filed or five business days passed).
- Who evaluates the quantitative tests, and how the result (`ci_results`, an input of 03 and 17A) is recorded and tied to its inputs.
- How a qualitative test's free-text `where` and its `lookback` become the documents the judge (14T) reads, when some of what `where` names (call transcripts, regulators' decisions, other companies' filings) is not on EDGAR or not the company's.
- What 16A extracts from a quarterly update ("update mode"), and who cuts the `source_excerpt` 04A's prompt says the pipeline supplies.
- How 03's outputs reach the archives: 00 §F2 names actions the runner did not know (`front_matter`, `append`, `patch`, `merge` with the public/private split of `sources_additions`, `pr_body`, `pr_attachment`), and 03 hands back complete new versions of `thesis.yml` and `ledger.yml`, which `place` refused to overwrite.
- How §G9 trust routing (below level 2, a quarterly update stays in the private repository until HQ has reviewed it) is carried out.
- How a chain of eight to twelve steps runs with human review between the draft, the audit and placement, without running anything twice.
- How to test all of this before the first event exists on EDGAR.
- Phase B: which documents the settler (15B) reads, where a settlement is written (SPEC 2.1 names a settlement file the pipeline fills), what the red team (04B-lite), the divergence map (14B) and HQ's gate (17A) receive, and how the gate's decision steers the chain.
- Two loops the design describes inside one event: "04A runs once more, on the new and changed facts only" after the revision, and a second revision when HQ returns an update. The runner kept one bundle per step, company and period.
- The size of 14T's input: in the Phase A rehearsal APP's four quarters of filings named by its tests' `where` came to about 2.2 MB of text (about 554k tokens).
- Two problems found by the model comparison of 2026-09-27 (`work/model-eval/RESULTS.md`, decision 0025): the claude-code backend reported an output-limit overrun as tool use, and replies whose YAML parsed but did not follow the part's format (04A's verdicts nested under a key, `must_fix` for `must fix`) were accepted.

## Options

1. **The event's documents:** (a) reuse `latest_filings`; (b) the event's own results documents (the 8-K's EX-99 exhibits and the 10-Q/10-K, or the results 6-K), refused until the event is on EDGAR and closed.
2. **Evaluating the quantitative tests:** (a) in the pipeline; (b) in thesis-ci, the owner of the test format, through a small interface, with the pipeline fetching companyfacts, converting 16B's extractions into readings and recording the evaluation like a run.
3. **Resolving `where`:** (a) a curated document list per test; (b) a model that reads `where` and picks documents; (c) a deterministic reader of form names over the company's own EDGAR filings that reports everything else as not supplied.
4. **16A on an update:** (a) the whole archive again; (b) the draft's outputs that carry facts, with the changes to `thesis.yml` and `ledger.yml` as diffs.
5. **Source excerpts for 04A:** (a) none; (b) cut by the pipeline: the sentence or table row of the cited document that contains the fact's value.
6. **Placing 03:** (a) plain writes only; (b) every §F2 action, with the replaced files guarded.
7. **§G9:** (a) left to the operator; (b) staged in the private repository by `place`, published after HQ's review by `place --publish`.
8. **Running an event:** (a) one command per step, by hand; (b) an event command that runs what is due, stops for review at fixed points, and resumes.
9. **Before the first event:** (a) nothing can be run end to end; (b) a dry-run rehearsal in which the last reported quarter stands in for the event, declared as such.
10. **Output structure:** (a) YAML parsing only; (b) the shape and fixed words of the YAML outputs later steps read, checked before the output is accepted.
11. **The claude-code output limit:** (a) keep 64k and the tools error; (b) the model's full ceiling, and a second turn without tools reported as a truncation.
12. **Settlements:** (a) written into the items file; (b) a settlement file per pre-registration period, `companies/<T>/prereg/<period>.settlement.yml` (SPEC 2.1), with the header the pipeline knows and the settler's results.
13. **A second round of a step in one event:** (a) run the same bundle again; (b) a second bundle of the step (`-r2`), with the round in the manifest.
14. **14T's documents:** (a) whole filings; (b) the sections each test's `where` names, cut at the filing's own headings, with the whole document as the fallback.
15. **17A for candidates:** (a) holdings only; (b) every quarterly update, holdings and candidates.

## Decision

1(b), 2(b), 3(c), 4(b), 5(b), 6(b), 7(b), 8(b), 9(b), 10(b), 11(b), 12(b), 13(b), 14(b), 15(b).

- **Steps.** New `StepSpec`s: `16B`, `14T`, `14A`, `03-draft`, `16A`, `04A`, `03R` (prompt part labels as `llm.prompt_part()` gives them). `StepSpec` gains `variables` (04's `{{subject}}` is `quarterly update`, which also makes 04A's `findings` a PR attachment), `post_event` (the step reads an event that must be on EDGAR and closed) and `about_company` (an HQ step about one company is stored as `runs/hq/<run_date>-<step>-<TICKER>/`, the name the phase-0 17A runs already use). Upstream outputs are the latest succeeded run of the step for the same company and period (`RunContext.latest_run`). Phase B adds `15B`, `04B-lite`, `14B` and `17A`.
- **The event's documents** (`filings`, `event`): `EdgarGateway.period_results()` finds the event of the period through `edgar.earnings_events()` and refuses (`EventNotReleased`) before the release and before the close; the event record gives the release date, the close date and the periodic report. Earnings call transcripts are not on EDGAR (STATUS T17); the document says so.
- **The evaluation (the `ci` step):** `python -m pipeline.runner evaluate <TICKER> <PERIOD> --run-date D` writes `runs/<TICKER>/<run_date>-ci/` like a run: `inputs/thesis.yml` and `inputs/readings.yml`, `manifest.yml` (sources: the XBRL companyfacts URL and sha256, the 16B runs used; the evaluator), `outputs/ci_results.yml` and `run.yml` (backend `pipeline`, no requests, the result counts). thesis-ci provides the logic through `thesis_ci.metrics.readings_from_companyfacts(companyfacts, metric_ids, periods, fiscal_year_end, *, ticker, definitions)` and `thesis_ci.evaluate.evaluate_company(thesis, readings, period, today)` (released in v0.4.0; `requirements.txt` pins the same tag as `requirements-lint.txt`, so the private Actions route and CI install it); `today` is the run date, so the result is reproducible. The pipeline fetches companyfacts through `pipeline.edgar` (cached for the submissions TTL), passes the XBRL metric ids, and turns 16B's `metric_values` into readings: a value that is not a number, a period that is not a fiscal period or a source without a tag is not a reading (it is listed as not usable, with the extractor's note, so the reason for an undetermined test is visible). The evaluation refuses to run while a due text metric has no 16B run. Tests that must not depend on thesis-ci's rules use a local shim of the interface (`tests/evaluate_shim.py`); a dry run whose thesis-ci lacks the interface uses a placeholder that records every test as undetermined, and the real `evaluate` refuses.
- **16B** gets `filings` and `metric_definitions`: the definitions (from the test's `metric_def`, else `spec/metrics.yml`) of the metrics the due tests read from filing text, without thresholds, claims or notes (§G6: the extractor sees no thesis). A test's `params.segment` or `params.holders` is carried in the id in brackets (`segment_revenue_yoy[Transaction services]`), and the readings get the matching `segment` or `series`. `prior_values` is the previous period's 16B output. Metrics not due this period (`first_readable` later than the run date) are listed as not read.
- **14T** gets the due qualitative tests (id, question, fail_if, warn_if, judge_notes, and the baseline's value, unit, period, as-of date and source; claim, note and the baseline's note removed) and `where_documents` from `pipeline/documents.py`: `where` is read clause by clause for the company's own filings (earnings 8-Ks and their EX-99 exhibits, other 8-Ks by item, 10-Q, 10-K, 6-K, 20-F, proxy statements, Forms 3/4/5, Schedules 13D/G, merger filings). Periodic reports are taken by fiscal period over the `lookback` quarters, the annual report and proxy in force as of the event are always included when named, and current reports are taken by filing date over the window (ownership reports capped at the latest 40). Every clause that names something else (transcripts, regulators, courts, legislatures, other issuers, the web) is reported as not supplied, with the reason, so the judge knows it cannot answer "no" on an incomplete set. Tags follow SPEC 3.3, with `-2`, `-3` for the second and later filing of one form on one day. From each periodic report only the sections the tests name are kept (MD&A, risk factors, legal proceedings, the business or regulation section, the financial statements, the named notes), cut at the filing's own headings (the form's item headings; a note by its number or title in the financial statements); a note the filing does not have is recorded as not in the filing, a note whose heading is not found falls back to the financial statements, and a section not found by any heading keeps the whole document. The manifest records, per document, the sections kept or why the whole document was kept. Forms 3, 4 and 5 are rendered from their XML as one line per transaction date, security, code and direction, without prices. Measured on the rehearsals: APP's 14T request about 257k tokens (from about 554k), PDD's about 245k; PDD-L1 names the whole 20-F, so it keeps the annual report entire.
- **03 draft** gets every input its front matter declares. A candidate's `qualitative_verdicts`, `prereg_*` and `question_list` are left out with the prompt's reasons; `ledger_settlement` and the `pending_*` inputs are explicit empty documents when nothing is due or waiting (pending items are those handed back since the last placed 03R/03P); `owner_notes` is `companies/<T>/owner_notes.md` in the private repository when it exists. The §G8 fields of `thesis.yml` and the ledger's `company` are pipeline fields.
- **16A (update mode)** gets `product`: the draft's `update`, `pr_body`, `question_answers`, `dossier_changes`, `mistakes_entry`, `story` and `sources_additions`, the changes to `thesis.yml` and `ledger.yml` as unified diffs against the current files, and the source table. **04A** gets `fact_table` with a `source_excerpt` cut for each fact from the cited document (this event's filings, the company's filings registered in `sources.yml` with an accession, the owner's report page for its `RPT` tags), or `excerpt_missing: true`; and `sources`: both source tables and the primary documents, written as `.txt`. **03R** gets `draft_outputs`, `findings_04A` (every audit round of the event), `sources` (with the draft's `sources_additions` and the documents the findings cite), for holdings `test_proposals_04B_lite`, and `returns_17A` when HQ returned the update.
- **15B** (the settler; holdings' pre-registration items and any company's due ledger entries) gets `event`; `items_blind`: the items due by the end of the month in which the event closes, with only id, statement, criterion, data source and horizon (no probability, author or book), sorted by id; `ledger_due`: the due ledger entries with the side, probability and status removed and `settle_as` added (`four_tier` for management's commitments, `binary` for the system's and the owner's forecasts); `metric_values`: the readings the evaluation used (XBRL and 16B, each with its source) and 16B's extraction as written; `filings`: the event's documents and the documents each item's `data_source` names, from its registration to this event (at most eight quarters), through the same resolver as 14T. It runs when something is due, before the draft, which reads its outputs.
- **Settlement files** (the placement action `settlement`): `prereg_settlement` is written as one `companies/<T>/prereg/<period>.settlement.yml` per pre-registration period of the items settled, validated against thesis-ci's `prereg-settlement.schema.json`. The pipeline writes the header (company, period, the results filing's acceptance time and accession from the event record, `merged_at` from the first commit of the items file, `ots_proof` when the proof exists) and each result's `settled_at` (the run date); a missing `source` is taken from the evidence's tag. A result already settled `happened` or `not_happened` is never rewritten (a different outcome is refused); an `undetermined` one may be settled later. `ledger_settlement` feeds the draft, which updates the ledger.
- **04B-lite** (holdings) gets `update` (the draft's update record), `filings`, `thesis_without_loss_paths` and `prior_inversion_list` (the latest earlier period's list, omitted for the first). Its `inversion_list` is a PR attachment and its `test_proposals` go to 03R.
- **14B** (holdings) gets `question_list`, `question_answers` (the draft's), `blind_answers` and `unprompted_observations` (14A), `filings`, `thesis`, `dossier` and `update`. Its `divergence_map` is a PR attachment.
- **17A** (HQ's review and release gate, for every quarterly update) gets `update_outputs` (every output of the latest 03R), `findings_04A` (all rounds), `inversion_list` and `divergence_map` (for a candidate, explicit documents saying the steps run only for holdings), `ci_results`, `trust_level` (from `thesis.yml` and `trust/levels.yml`, the lower governs, with the route of `decision-rights.yml`), `gate_rules` (the gate's blocking conditions and routing, with what the pipeline already knows about each: must-fix findings of every audit round and what the revision notes did with them, failed breaker tests from `ci` and 14T, divergences touching a pillar, the trust level; lint runs when the update is placed), `questions` (every question raised in the event, by run), `valuation_input_notes` (when a valuation input changed; optional) and `decision_rights`. The chain reads `gate_decision` (`release`, `return` or `hold`).
- **Rounds** (`registry.ROUNDS`: 16A, 04A, 03R and 17A may run twice in one event): a second round is a second bundle, `<run_date>-<step>[-TICKER]-r2`, with `round: 2` in the manifest; `latest_run` orders by round, run date and name. 16A's second round gets `product` as the unified diffs of what 03R changed in the draft's fact-carrying outputs (run only when it changed one), and 04A's second round audits that extraction; 03R's second round gets the first revision as `draft_outputs` and HQ's `returns_17A` as must-fix, and 17A's second round reviews it. A superseded revision is not placed.
- **Placement actions (§F2):** `write`; `front_matter` (`reviewed_sections` into the update record); `append` (the mistakes entry, added as the newest under "## The list"); `merge` (`sources_additions` split by visibility into each repository's `sources.yml`, appended in the file's own indentation; a registered tag with different fields is refused, never rewritten); `patch` (`dossier_changes`, part by part by the dossier's fixed headings, `version_history` entries appended; kept in the run directory while no dossier exists); `pr_body` (03's body followed by the PR attachments of the same event: 04A's findings, 14T's rulings, 04B-lite's inversion list and 14B's divergence map, kept as `pr_body.md` in the run directory and checked for §H4 wording with thesis-ci's lists); `pr_attachment` (nothing written; attached through the PR body). `thesis.yml`, `ledger.yml` and `story.md` are replaced only while the current file is the one the update read (the sha256 recorded by the 03 draft's manifest). The draft and the `ci` step are never placed.
- **§G9:** for 03R and 03P, the trust level is `thesis.yml`'s (the lower of it and `trust/levels.yml`'s, with a warning when they differ). At level 2 or above the public files go to the public branch; below it they are written to `runs/<scope>/<run_dir>/staged/<public path>` in the private repository, complete as they would be published; `place --publish` after HQ's review writes them to the public branch, and refuses when what would be published now differs from the staged copy HQ reviewed. Level 0 adds a warning for the letter. The publication is recorded in `publication.yml`.
- **The event chain** (`pipeline/chain.py`; `python -m pipeline.runner event <TICKER> <PERIOD> --run-date D [--dry-run] [--approve draft|audit|placement] [--retry]`): 16B (when a due test reads filing text), ci, [14T when a qualitative test is due, 14A], 15B (when a prediction or ledger entry is due), 03 draft, stop *draft*, 16A, 04A, [04B-lite, 14B], stop *audit*, 03R, 16A-r2 and 04A-r2 (when the revision changed a fact-carrying output), 17A, 03R-r2 and 17A-r2 (when HQ returned the update), stop *placement* (it shows HQ's decision; a second return fails the chain there for HQ to resolve by hand), place (bracketed: holdings only). Each call runs what is due until a stop, a failure or the end; a succeeded bundle is never run again, whatever its run date; a failed one stops the chain until `--retry`; a stop passes once `--approve` has written `review.yml` (stop, reviewer, time) into the bundle under review. The exit status is 0 when complete, 3 at a stop, 1 on a failure; one line per stage is printed, never content. A step not built yet stops the chain with that message.
- **Rehearsal:** a dry run (`dry-run`, `evaluate --out`, `event --dry-run`) of a post-event step whose event is not on EDGAR yet uses the last reported quarter's documents, marked `substitute` in the manifest with a note, and the manifest says `rehearsal: true`; a real assembly refuses a rehearsal. A dry run looks for upstream runs in its own directory first, and takes the unplaced question list of a 14Q dry run there.
- **Output structure** (`outputs.STRUCTURES`, `outputs.structure_errors()`): the YAML outputs no thesis-ci schema covers but a later step reads are checked for their shape (a list of rows, or a mapping with a list), the keys every row carries, and their fixed words exactly as the rules write them: 04A's `fact_verdicts` (accurate, consistent with citation, error, L2 only, unconfirmed, basis issue) and every part's `findings` (§F3 groups and types; a no-change finding has type null) and `questions` (§F8); `revision_notes` (§F4); 16A's `fact_table`; 16B's `metric_values`; 14T's `qualitative_verdicts`; 15B's settlements; 03's `dossier_changes`, `reviewed_sections` and `question_answers`; 14A's `blind_answers`; 14B's `divergence_map`; 17A's `gate_decision`. A malformed reply takes the existing one retry with the precise error.
- **The claude-code backend** gets the model's full output ceiling for every part (128000; 64000 for `claude-haiku-4-5`): the CLI's limit covers thinking and the reply. A CLI run with more than one turn but no tool loaded and no permission asked is the CLI continuing after that limit, and is raised as `LLMTruncated` with the reason, not as a tools error.

## Rationale

- **The event's own documents, and only after the close:** 03 runs "once at the close, with all the materials supplied at once"; an update drafted on the press release alone would be redone when the 10-Q arrives.
- **thesis-ci evaluates:** the rules (`rule`, `consecutive`, `evaluate_on`, units) are thesis-ci's format; evaluating them anywhere else would fork the semantics, and a public, tested engine lets others use it. Recording the evaluation like a run (inputs, hashes, evaluator version, the run date as `today`) makes `ci_results` reproducible from the bundle alone, as model outputs are.
- **A deterministic `where` reader that says what it could not supply:** a model choosing documents would be one more unaudited judgment in front of the judge; a curated list per test would go stale. The judge's rule ("no" only with every required document) needs to know what is missing more than it needs everything.
- **Update mode and pipeline-cut excerpts:** the audit checks what this update asserts, not the archive again; the excerpt puts the source sentence next to each fact, as the 04A prompt describes, and `excerpt_missing` flags where the auditor must look harder.
- **Guarded replacement:** replacing `thesis.yml` is what an update does, but a file edited by hand after the draft read it must not be overwritten silently.
- **Staging inside the private repository:** §G9 says the update "stays in the private repository first"; staging the complete files lets HQ review exactly what will be published, and the publication check makes sure nothing else is.
- **Stops recorded in the bundle:** the review is part of the record, next to what was reviewed; resuming from the files on disk means a crash or a pause costs nothing and nothing is paid for twice.
- **Rehearsals:** the chain, the placement plan and the document resolver run on real EDGAR data before the event, so the first real run is not the first run.
- **Structure checks:** the next step, the placement and the error lists depend on these words; catching a malformed reply while the retry is still available costs one call, catching it later costs a manual repair.
- **Settlement files written by the pipeline:** SPEC 2.1 keeps the items file unchanged after its timestamp; the settlement header (acceptance time, accession, merge time, proof) is what the pipeline knows and the settler must not be trusted to copy. A settled result never changes, as the pre-registration is only worth its immutability.
- **Blind inputs for 15B:** the settler decides whether a statement happened from the criterion and the data; the probability, the author and the ledger side would tell it what the book hoped for.
- **Second rounds as second bundles:** a bundle is the record of one call; running it again would overwrite what the first round's reviewers saw. The second audit on the diff only keeps the rule's "new and changed facts only" and its cost small.
- **One return loop:** a second return means the company manager and HQ disagree on something a revision cannot settle; HQ's rulings are the place for it, not a third model round.
- **17A for candidates:** the gate (lint, must-fix findings, breakers, trust routing) applies to every public update, and prompt 17 has no scope limit; the candidate simply has no inversion list or divergence map, and the documents say so.
- **Sections by the filing's headings:** the tests name sections; the headings are the filing's own, so the cut is reproducible and auditable, and the fallback keeps the judge's "no only with every required document" rule safe.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Reusing `latest_filings`:** it is the quarter before the one being pre-registered, not the event being reported.
- **Evaluating in the pipeline:** a second implementation of thesis-ci's rules.
- **Recomputing ci_results inside 03's assembly:** the evaluation would not be a recorded, reviewable step, and 17A would have to recompute it again.
- **A model resolving `where`:** unaudited, and not reproducible from the bundle.
- **Leaving §G9 to the operator:** the routing is a rule, and forgetting it once publishes an unreviewed update.
- **Approvals as command-line flags only, not recorded:** the record would not show who passed a stop, or when.
- **Relaxing `llm.py`'s tools check for multi-turn runs without saying why:** a truncated reply would be accepted as complete.
- **Settlement results in the items file:** it would change after its timestamp (SPEC 2.1).
- **The settler writing the header:** a model copying accession numbers and times is an avoidable source of errors.
- **Re-running a bundle for the second round:** the first round's record would be lost.
- **A model choosing the sections for 14T:** the same objection as to a model resolving `where`.
- **Unlimited return loops:** the chain would pay for rounds that do not converge.

## What remains

- 03P (the patch merge between events) is not wired.
- Other issuers' filings named in `where` and call transcripts (T17) are reported as not supplied.
- A third audit round (after 03R-r2) is not run; 17A-r2 reviews the second revision with the audit rounds before it.
- The private staging path for level-1 updates (`runs/<T>/<run>/staged/<public path>`) is to be written into SPEC (thesis-ci backlog T23(2)).
- The first real event (AXP FY2026Q3, around 2026-10-14) replaces the rehearsal; the rehearsals only exercise the fake backend.
