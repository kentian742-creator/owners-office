# Masters' principles → system rules

A Chinese version is in [zh-CN/constitution/masters.md](../zh-CN/constitution/masters.md).

> In Owner's Office these investors' philosophies are **organizing principles, not personas**. The system has no "Buffett agent" or "Munger agent", and nobody imitates their voice to give opinions; there is only an office run the Berkshire way: divided authority, audits, calibration and trust levels. Public descriptions do not use "masters' methodology" or "multi-agent adversarial" as selling points either.
>
> Investment beliefs are written only in [owner.md](owner.md) (R1–R13, H1–H5). This page only explains what mechanism each principle became, which constitution or governance clause it rests on, and which check enforces it. The first three columns are copied from the design document. In the "Clauses" column, R and H numbers refer to owner.md, and §G refers to the governance clauses of series rules 00 (private repository, cited by number); checks are defined in thesis-ci's `spec/checks.yml`.

## Mechanisms without a check yet (to-do)

- **Candor:** "bad news first" in the letter to the owner currently relies only on HQ writing to the structure of 18. A check of the letter's structure should be registered in thesis-ci and implemented together with the first monthly letter.
- **Switch the template when the category changes:** there are only the template files for now; nothing checks that `category` agrees with the template tests (`origin: template:<category>`).
- **18 months as the main horizon:** C-SCHEMA only ensures that each pre-registration item states a horizon; "at least half `18m`" is ensured by the pre-registration's self-check before delivery, with no registered check.
- **Circle of competence:** "domains with poor calibration do not enter the candidates" can be enforced only in phase 4, once enough settled forecasts have accumulated; until then only the trend is read, and no conclusions are drawn.
- C-TRUST-WRITE, C-PROMPT-ISOLATION and C-PREREG-IMMUTABLE were added with thesis-ci spec 0.2; until their implementation lands, the rows above that cite them have a mechanism but no check.
